PDFguna Privacy Policy

Effective September 22, 2026

PDFguna is an Android app for opening, merging, splitting, and editing PDF files on your device. This policy explains what the app processes and what it does not.

Contact: hataketr@gmail.com

Your PDF files

PDF contents, images, and processed results stay on your device. PDFguna does not upload those files to our servers. Files you save remain in your device storage, including the download folder you choose.

Data stored on your device

The app stores settings locally, such as dark mode, file view, the last page you read, and your default save folder. This data is not sent to PDFguna servers. Uninstalling the app removes it from the device.

Device permissions

  • Storage, so you can choose PDFs and save the results.
  • Internet and network state, used only to show ads.

Advertising

PDFguna shows ads through Google AdMob. Google may process data such as your advertising ID, device type, IP address, and ad interactions in order to serve and measure ads. PDFguna does not sell your files or PDF contents to advertisers.

Google’s privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

How Google uses information from sites and apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Children

PDFguna is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

This policy may be updated. The date at the top shows the current version.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to hataketr@gmail.com.
